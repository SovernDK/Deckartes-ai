Deckartes privacy notice

Last updated 14 November 2026

Deckartes is a personal, non-commercial assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. Its bank connection, through Enable Banking, is used only by the owner, for the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

What is read

  • The accounts the owner approves at their bank: account names and currencies.
  • Their transactions for the dates asked about: date, amount, direction, counterparty name, description and status.

Nothing is ever changed at the bank: the access is read-only and no payments can be made.

Where it goes

  • Transactions are fetched only when the owner asks a question that needs them, or once a week and once a month for the owner's spending summary, and are read by a language model running locally on the owner's computer. They are not sent to any AI provider.
  • The answers are kept in the owner's local chat history on the same computer.
  • If the owner asks through their own Telegram bot, the answer passes through Telegram's servers to the owner's phone.
  • The bank link (Enable Banking's session id and the account list) is stored on the owner's computer, readable only by them.
  • Each payee's spending category and each finished week's and month's totals per category are stored on the owner's computer, and a weekly and monthly summary of them is sent to the owner's own Telegram bot.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, used for advertising or sent to any other party.

How long

The bank's approval lasts until it expires (at most 180 days) or is withdrawn at the bank or in Enable Banking. Local chat history can be deleted at any time from the app.

Contact

Questions go to the owner through the project's GitHub page.
